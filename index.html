How the score works

Each scoring window starts with a 10 and progressively loses points for heat, rain, thunderstorms, muggy heat, strong sun, gusts, wet roads and poor air quality. Humidity only counts with heat (80% from 28°C, 85% from 30°C), and humidity, dew point and heat stress share one penalty so they don't stack. Thunderstorms or dangerous heat cap it at 4. Separate notes will appear for people with asthma or heart conditions and will not change the overall score.
